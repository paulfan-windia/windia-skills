## Start a review

> Use WINDIA QS Piling Final Account Review Tool Lite. Review the initial file inventory and provide a first-pass closeout brief. Flag incomplete revisions, possible missing pile records and questions for the quantity surveyor.

Original pile installation logs and the submitted final-account schedule, latest approved pile layout and foundation or pile-cap details, survey datums and levels, approved changes, site sign-offs, the executed contract, the BOQ, and adopted measurement rules. Confirm the project zero datum, embedment, units, and separate quantity and level tolerances. Add drive-down, cut-off, pile-segment and splice or weld records, and the basis for payable length or supported weight where relevant.

For the closeout of residential, industrial and commercial construction packages involving driven precast square piles, prestressed high-strength concrete (PHC) spun piles, and driven steel pipe piles with verifiable installation and measurement records. Bored and other cast-in-place piles are out of scope. Ask for a scope note, a source checklist, issue headings and a review workbook outline. Closed-out reconciliation and formal sign-off require the Full edition and the authorised project team.
