# WINDIA QS Quantity Takeoff & BOQ Tool for Residential Common-Area Finishes (Lite)

**WINDIA QS Common-Area Takeoff & BOQ Tool (Lite)**

Scope a residential common-area finishes takeoff before tender. Turn your drawing register and project requirements into a preliminary BOQ outline, missing-information list and review plan.

## What it does

Organise residential lobbies, corridors and stair lobbies for a finishes takeoff. Identify missing revisions, ambiguous boundaries or finish schedules and prepare a consistent tender BOQ structure.

## What to provide

Supply a drawing list and revisions, finish schedules, scope and tender instructions, addenda and any BOQ headings.

## What you get

Receive a preliminary drawing register, coverage checklist, scope questions and BOQ outline. Suggested reference fields identify the source drawing, revision, location and unresolved question.
