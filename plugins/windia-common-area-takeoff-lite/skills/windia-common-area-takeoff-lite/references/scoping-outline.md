# Scope and intake outline

Track the drawing ID, revision and approval status alongside finish schedules, typical details and tender addenda. Outline floors, walls, ceilings, trims and confirmed fixture coverage. Record each gap, reference and reviewer action in a preliminary BOQ skeleton.

| Field | Record |
| --- | --- |
| Scope | Building, level, area and trade boundary |
| Drawing | Number, revision and location |
| BOQ outline | Section, description, unit, status and query |

BOQ means bill of quantities. RICS is the Royal Institution of Chartered Surveyors. NRM 2 addresses detailed measurement. FIDIC publishes standard contract forms; check the relevant project edition and amendments. Bid levelling compares submitted bids on a common scope and basis.
