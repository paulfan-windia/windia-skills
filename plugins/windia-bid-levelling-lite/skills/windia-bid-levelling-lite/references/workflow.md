## The six-step workflow

1. **Collect source documents.** List the package, instructions to tenderers, BOQ revision, addenda, submitted pricing and commercial schedules. Record what is available and who owns each gap.
2. **Proofread and verify.** Note visible gaps or version conflicts and identify the confirmed comparison basis if supplied. Mark uncertain scope, dates and currency for confirmation.
3. **Reorganise.** Draft a review outline under scope, price comparison, commercial responses and clarification. Use placeholders for evidence and findings.
4. **Redact and check permissions.** Use bidder aliases in shared materials. Summarise sensitive items and keep the outline within the authorised audience.
5. **Render.** Produce an English draft brief or blank template skeleton with headings and placeholders. Apply the edition notice to every document, export and reply.
6. **Consolidate into the Skill.** Preserve reusable headings and generic intake questions. Omit project content from reusable materials.
