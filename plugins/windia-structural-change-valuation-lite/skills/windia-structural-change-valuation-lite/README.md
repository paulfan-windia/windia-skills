# WINDIA QS AI Structural Change Order Valuation Tool (Lite)

Review quantities and cost estimates for structural changes during construction.

Scope a structural change order during construction. Organise baseline and revised documents, flag missing evidence, and outline a quantity and cost comparison for QS review.

## What it does

Organise an initial post-contract review when changes to wall, column or support details may affect quantities and costs.

## What to provide

Supply baseline and revised drawing references, the change notice or instruction, an outline scope, and the BOQ or rate schedule index.

## What you get

A document register, scope queries, and a blank before-and-after valuation outline. Record proposed drawing and rate reference fields for a reviewer to complete.

## About WINDIA

Developed by a team led by a Member of the Royal Institution of Chartered Surveyors (MRICS). The supporting engineering consultancy group has over 20 years of experience, with Wanzhonglong Project Management Co., Ltd. as its mainland China operation, an international headquarters in Hong Kong, and a Southeast Asia operations centre in Thailand. The group has delivered engineering consultancy and project management services on more than 6,000 projects for leading developers and manufacturers.

The WINDIA QS AI Skill series serves quantity surveyors, cost consultants, EPC contractors and owner teams across construction, real estate, commercial and manufacturing projects. Planned releases cover bid levelling, contract risk, interim valuations and final accounts, AI-assisted takeoff, BOQ rate build-ups, estimate checks, cost benchmarking and consistent reporting. Planned regional tools support comparison of Chinese and Thai measurement rules and BOQ preparation in Chinese, English and Thai.
