## What it is

A free, MIT-licensed starter for an evidence-led Quantity Surveying tender intake and a focused Bid Review brief. It helps the user establish what can be reviewed and the next actions.

## Who it serves and scope

QS practitioners, tender teams and engineering consultants starting a Tender Proposal, Cost Audit or Construction Cost review. Accept tender instructions, evaluation criteria, BOQ, draft contract and current addenda. Identify whether the user is preparing a consultant's bid or reviewing a contractor's submission.

## Output and method

1. Record the project, package, stage and intended decision, then list supplied sources with revision and location.
2. Check the stated scope, current amendments, start and finish conditions, deliverables, measurement basis, team evidence and submission rules. Identify the applicable RICS measurement method and FIDIC contract edition when supplied.
3. Record each finding with the source, effect, evidence required and next owner or action. Keep unresolved contradictions visible.
4. Use [the one-page template](templates/review-brief.md) to return the source summary, key issues, initial proposal outline and next actions. State the actual status supported by the available information.
5. Recalculate any small arithmetic used in the brief and state the unit, currency and tax basis. [Mini example](examples/mini-review.md).

The user's current request and project evidence govern what is drafted. Apply contract-specific measurement, valuation and authority provisions when making a recommendation. The user reviews the brief before relying on it in the tender process.
