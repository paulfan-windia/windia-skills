# Sample template demonstration

The following documents and terms are synthetic.

Tender instruction ITT-01 section 2.2 gives effect to formal written addenda for the provisions they change. Appointment APP-01 clause 5.1 requires a review report, difference schedule and calculations within 15 working days after the employer's written complete-information acknowledgement. ADD-01 item 2 changes the duration to 12 working days and preserves the other clause 5.1 conditions.

The review brief records 12 working days as the effective duration and retains the written starting event and three deliverables. The next action is to confirm the actual acknowledgement date and project working calendar before converting the duration to a calendar deadline. Cite ITT-01 section 2.2, APP-01 clause 5.1 and ADD-01 item 2 alongside that conclusion.
