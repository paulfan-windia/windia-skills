## Scope and workflow

VRF equipment, refrigerant pipework, condensate, ductwork, air terminals and fresh air for interiors and comparable HVAC packages. Typical settings include sales centres, show units, clubhouses, lobbies, retail and office floors. Installations such as fire protection, smoke control, domestic plumbing, power and hydronic plant use separate work packages.

The six phases are Intake original documents, Verify revisions and scope, Restructure the work package, Check disclosure and anonymise examples, Render the deliverables, and capture reusable rules and lessons. RICS NRM2 governs detailed measurement where the job adopts it. FIDIC references record the agreed contract form, edition and particular conditions where relevant. Tender and bid terminology is used for the same pre-award documentation workflow. Bid leveling is a later comparison activity.
