| Area | Starting point | Useful question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Drawing register | Title, number, revision, discipline, approval | Which revision controls this tender? |
| Scope | Zones, suites or floors, services and exclusions | Which work is owner-supplied or by other trades? |
| Schedule | Equipment, terminal IDs, pipe and duct tags | Which clarifications are still open? |
| BOQ setup | Description, package, unit placeholder, drawing reference, notes | Who approves the scope and measurement basis? |

Before takeoff, list the inputs and uncertainties that require review. Use the terms quantity takeoff, bill of quantities (BOQ), CAD drawing register and scope clarification. Record whether the project adopts a RICS measurement method or a FIDIC contract form. Bid leveling concerns comparing contractor bids after submissions.
