### Preliminary tender takeoff brief

**Project:** Synthetic developer sales centre. **Purpose:** Prepare a scoped HVAC takeoff brief and a BOQ structure for the QS.

| Review area | What to record | Decision before takeoff |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Drawings | HVAC plan revision B and corresponding equipment schedule | Confirm that the tender clarification did not supersede either drawing. |
| VRF routes | Indoor and outdoor units, refrigerant services and approved system boundaries | Ask the designer to confirm whether the outside riser is in this package. |
| Ventilation | Ductwork, fresh-air pipe and supply diffusers | Request the agreed method for describing size changes and separate fittings. |
| Condensate | Pipework, route and connection boundary | Ask who takes responsibility for the receiving drainage connection. |

Draft BOQ headings: equipment, refrigerant pipework, condensate drainage, ductwork, supply terminals, fresh-air services, and tender qualifications. Reference placeholders: drawing ID, revision, zone, trade boundary and review status. Quantity fields remain blank, and the reviewer records the missing information before selecting the final takeoff boundary.
