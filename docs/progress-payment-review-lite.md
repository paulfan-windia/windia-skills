# WINDIA QS Construction Progress Payment Review Tool (Lite)

This is the free WINDIA QS Lite edition. For the complete rules, working papers, detailed checks and formal deliverables, search for WINDIA QS on the Agensi.io Skill Marketplace. Contact: esofanwentao@gmail.com.

Review work completed, reconcile cumulative and current-period valuations, and produce a traceable Excel workbook.

Prepare a preliminary construction progress payment review for monthly payment applications, progress claims and interim valuations. Organize the submitted records, identify missing supporting evidence and outline a consistent review. Use a signed contract, a priced BOQ or schedule of values, the prior accepted valuation, current site progress records and the variation register. The output contains an intake register, open questions and a blank review outline. Source pointers identify the relevant revision and location for follow-up. Detailed checks, calculations, working papers and production deliverables are included in Full.

Use this tool after contract award for building works and specialist packages, including residential, commercial and industrial projects. It is intended for quantity surveyors, project controllers, client-side cost teams, contract administrators, general contractors and EPC commercial teams. The contract and payment schedule govern each assessment.

## Industry terms

Payment application, progress claim and interim valuation are post-award terms. A BOQ or schedule of values identifies the payment lines. Bid levelling belongs to tender evaluation. Apply RICS NRM 2 only if the adopted measurement basis calls for it. Use the executed FIDIC form and Particular Conditions where applicable.
